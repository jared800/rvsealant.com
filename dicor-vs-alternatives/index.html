Dicor lap sealant vs the alternatives

Updated 2026-10-20

Dicor's lap sealant is the default for membrane roofs; Dicor calls it one of the highest-volume products sold in RV stores. The alternatives aren't better or worse in general. They solve different jobs.

When Dicor is the right call

For an EPDM, TPO or PVC roof, around vents, edges and screw heads, Dicor's self-leveling lap sealant is the purpose-built choice, with non-sag for vertical edges. Dicor also makes a gunnable butyl sealant for the primary seal under windows, vents, skylights and termination rails.

Flat roof joints

Dicor Self-Leveling Lap Sealant

Edges, vents, vent pipes, screw heads.

  • Lists EPDM, TPO and PVC.
  • Six colors, 10.3 oz tube.

Dicor Gunnable Butyl Sealant

Under window, vent and skylight flanges; termination rails.

  • Lists TPO, EPDM, PVC, glass, metals, aluminum.
  • Dries to a putty-like consistency; grey.
  • Dicor coverage: about 11 ft per tube at a 3/8" bead, 24 ft at 1/4".

When an alternative fits better

Fiberglass & aluminum

Geocel Pro Flex RV Flexible Sealant

Sidewalls, trim and tail lights rather than the roof membrane.

  • Paintable; can be applied over itself.
  • Adheres to damp surfaces per Geocel.

Not for EPDM or TPO, per Geocel.

Seams & tears

EternaBond RoofSeal tape

When a joint is long or a tear needs a patch.

  • Tape with a UV-stable backing.
  • Use EternaPrime below 40°F per the maker.

Metal roofs

Sashco Through the Roof!

Metal RV roofs and damp-surface leak repairs.

  • Sashco says it sticks to wet surfaces and can seal active leaks.
  • Clear, white or black.

Sashco says not to use it on rubber membranes such as EPDM.
