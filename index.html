Self-leveling vs non-sag lap sealant

Horizontal

Self-leveling

Flows and levels itself, so it fills around vent flanges, screw heads and flat roof edges. On a vertical or steep surface it will run. Example: Dicor 501LS series.

Vertical

Non-sag (non-leveling)

Holds its bead where you put it. Use it on front and rear caps, termination rails and anything that isn't flat. Example: Dicor 551LS series.

Dicor Self-Leveling Lap Sealant

Roof edges, air vents, vent pipes and screw heads.

Before you buy: three quick checks

  1. Know your roof. EPDM (rubber), TPO, fiberglass, aluminum/metal or a coated roof. Your owner's manual or the RV maker can tell you. See sealant by roof type.
  2. Check for silicone. Several makers (including EternaBond and RV Roof Magic) say their product won't bond over silicone. Old silicone has to come off first.
  3. Match the old sealant. If you are touching up, stay with a product that is compatible with what's already there, or remove the old bead down to a clean surface.
